A geospatial planning assistant for Puerto Rico communities. Five screens, each built around a real question a community member or planner would ask — and each showing the thing that makes the product defensible: every claim is labelled by how it was established, and traceable to the document or layer it came from.

B

Confidence taxonomy

The core design system

Not every statement in an answer is established the same way. A distance the system measured is not the same kind of fact as a zone classification read off an official layer. The interface labels each one, so a reader can tell at a glance what to rely on — and a single answer can carry more than one tier.

Verificado

Stated in an official source

Read directly from a government layer or quoted from an adopted plan. The strongest claim the tool makes.

EX · “Dentro de zona inundable FEMA 2009”
FUENTE · Capa oficial FEMA
Calculado

Measured by the system

Computed geometrically — distance, count, overlap. Accurate as arithmetic, but the method and its limits are stated.

EX · “2.4 km en línea recta”
MÉTODO · Distancia euclidiana, no por carretera
Inferido

Synthesized from document text

Summarized or interpreted from prose in a plan. Always paired with the passage it came from so a reader can check it.

EX · “El plan prioriza vivienda resiliente”
FUENTE · Plan de Mitigación, secc. 4.6
Sin dato

Not in the library

The documents do not answer it. The tool says so and routes the user onward — and shows no citations.

EX · “No hay información suficiente”
ACCIÓN · Remite a Junta de Planificación / OGPe

En lenguaje sencillo Inferido
Sí se puede construir un centro comunitario aquí, con tres condiciones:
  • La mitad del solar debe quedar libre. El edificio no puede cubrir más del 50% del terreno — en este solar de 2,180 m², eso son 1,090 m² máximo. [§6.2.4]
  • Hace falta estacionamiento. Un espacio por cada 50 m² construidos — unos 22 espacios si se construye al máximo. [§6.2.9]
  • No aplica elevación por inundación. Esta parcela queda fuera de zona inundable especial, así que §9.1.2 no le aplica. [§9.1.2]
Esta interpretación resume el texto citado a la izquierda. No sustituye una consulta formal con la OGPe ni una determinación de la Junta de Planificación.

C

Open questions for the team

Decisions needed

Confidence taxonomy — feasibility

Verified / computed / inferred is designed here. The system must be able to tell them apart before the UI can show them. Needs an engineering spike alongside the design.

Datasets not yet loaded

Screens 04 and 05 assume cadastre (CRIM), zoning districts and transit stops. Currently we hold hazard, facility and boundary layers plus 84 municipal plans.

Public access

NYU policy currently blocks anonymous access. Org-policy exception, or move the front end to the ClimateIQ pattern? Blocks any public launch.

Inventory reconciliation

84 plans are loaded from a government manifest. These should be matched against La Maraña's official inventory before launch.
